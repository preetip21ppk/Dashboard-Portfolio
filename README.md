# Dashboard Portfolio

A collection of Power BI dashboards built across internships, coursework, and independent projects — covering content analytics, hospitality, weather/air quality, customer analytics, HR, e-commerce, and sales forecasting.

## Dashboards

**Amazon Prime Content Analytics**
Analyzes 9,655 titles across genres, ratings, countries, and release trends.
`Tools: Power BI, DAX`

**Restaurant Ratings Analysis**
5-page report covering dining, hospitality, consumer behavior, and reviews for 130 restaurants.
`Tools: Power BI`

**Live Weather & Air Quality Dashboard**
Weather and AQI monitoring across 8 cities, built on an AWS data pipeline (S3, Glue, Athena, EC2).
`Tools: Power BI, Python, AWS`

**HR Analytics Dashboard**
Workforce attrition, tenure, and salary analysis across HR, R&D, and Sales departments.
`Tools: Power BI`

**Online Store Sales Dashboard**
Sales and profit by region, segment, and payment method, plus a 20-day sales forecast.
`Tools: Power BI`

**Customer Behavior Dashboard**
Customer segmentation by subscription status, category, and age group.
`Tools: Power BI`

**E-Commerce Sales Dashboard**
Sales and profit tracked by state, payment mode, and product category.
`Tools: Power BI`

**Airport Performance Dashboard**
Airport operations and performance analytics.
`Tools: Power BI`

## How to view

Each `.pbix` file can be opened directly in [Power BI Desktop](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-bi/desktop) (free).
